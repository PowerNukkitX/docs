Coming from another Bedrock server? Your worlds, settings and resource packs can usually come with you. Pick the software you are leaving:

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<Hammer />} title="Nukkit or PowerNukkit" href="/docs/migration/nukkit">
    Mostly automatic: PowerNukkitX converts your old configuration on first start.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Server />} title="Bedrock Dedicated Server" href="/docs/migration/bds">
    Mojang's official server. Worlds and resource packs come along; settings are copied by hand.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<CircleAlert />} title="PocketMine-MP" href="/docs/migration/pocketmine">
    A PHP server. Worlds come along, but plugins must be replaced.
  </Card>
</Cards>

<Callout type="warn" title="Back up first">
  Copy your whole server folder somewhere safe before you start, whatever you migrate from.
</Callout>
