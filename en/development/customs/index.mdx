PowerNukkitX has **registries** for custom content. You register a block, an item or an entity from your plugin, and the server serves it to Bedrock clients, with the textures and models coming from a resource pack.

## Register in `onLoad()`, not `onEnable()`

<PluginLifecycle highlight="onLoad" />

<Callout type="error" title="The most common mistake">
  The server reads the worlds, with their blocks, items and entities, **after** loading plugins but **before** enabling them. Register custom blocks, items, entities and enchantments in `onLoad()`: registered in `onEnable()`, they are unknown while the world loads, which causes loading errors or data loss.
</Callout>
