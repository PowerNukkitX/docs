The server decides what a custom block, item or entity **does**. What it **looks like** comes from a **resource pack** that players download when they join. Without it, players see missing textures.

## Where each kind gets its look

| Custom content | What the pack provides | Matches in your code |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| [Block](/docs/development/customs/blocks) | The texture, listed in `terrain_texture.json`, and its geometry for shaped blocks | `.texture("name")`, `Geometry("geometry.name")` |
| [Item](/docs/development/customs/items) | The texture, listed in `item_texture.json` | `.texture("name")` |
| [Entity](/docs/development/customs/entities) | Textures, the model and the client-side entity definition | the entity's identifier |

<Callout type="warn" title="Names must match exactly">
  The name you pass to `.texture(...)` must be the key in the pack's `terrain_texture.json` or `item_texture.json`. A typo shows as a missing texture, with no error on the server.
</Callout>

## Ship the pack with your server

<Steps>
  <Step>Package your pack as a `.mcpack` or `.zip`.</Step>
  <Step>Put it in the server's `resource_packs/` folder.</Step>
  <Step>Restart the server. It offers the pack to every player when they connect.</Step>
</Steps>

<Files>
  <Folder name="server" defaultOpen>
    <Folder name="plugins" defaultOpen>
      <File name="my-plugin.jar" />
    </Folder>
    <Folder name="resource_packs" defaultOpen>
